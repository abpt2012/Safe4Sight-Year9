Experience

How a very serious flood affected me and my family

I was only a young child when I experienced my first serious flood. I was at home, playing games and suddenly rain started pouring heavily. I didn't think much of it at first, but I slowly started to notice water seeping into my house.

I live in a one-story house, so I unfortunately had to experience this. I started getting worried when the water started seeping from the roof as well, and my parents told me we had to evacuate. We tried to reach to our family car, but the car itself was already submerged in water ! I was soaking wet, and I got a fever the next day.
