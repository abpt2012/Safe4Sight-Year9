Household assessment

Answer all 5 questions based on your current situation.

1
What is the main construction material of your house?

Choose the main material you know your house uses.

2
How would you describe the current condition of your house?

Look for visible cracks, damaged walls, roof condition, leaks, etc.

3
Do you have an emergency bag ready to take with you during an evacuation?

Think about supplies that are packed together and easy to carry.

4
Does your household have an emergency escape plan?

Think about the place everyone will meet up, if the way out of your house is always available, etc.

5
Are essential emergency supplies available in your household?

Supplies like drinking water, food, first aid, medicine, flashlights, and batteries.
